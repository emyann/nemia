A gate on the Factory says what it asks and takes the answer: the brief and Approve / Send back on the run's card, the gate's node and the inspector

The owner tried to approve a ship gate from the Factory on 2026-09-19 and could not. What the Factory showed for a run at its ending's gate: on the run's card, "to decide: Ship it: trial merge this run's result branch onto the base branch, run the check, and commit —…", cut off, with `to decide` and `waiting on you` chips; on the gate's node, "HUMAN GATE · ship · awaiting approval · no attempt yet · waiting on you · approve ship"; and in the step inspector, first a command line to copy — "approve it: wf approve <run> ship — or send it back: wf retry <run> review --message "<objection>"" — then a table of dashes (attempts 0, latest began —, latest ended —, latest took —, used —, cost —, branch —, task: not filed), then the step's spec. In the owner's words: "I have no information about what is being asked of me on the board. There is no button giving me the ability to approve or refuse … in the card we would want a very quick action button, like Approve or Reject, or Reject with a message. It should be on the card. The inspector detail is already a mess but I need somehow to know what is being asked of me."

The ability exists, one page away: apps/web/src/components/Gate.tsx (Approve with optional words, Send back with an objection, through `approveStep` and `askTry`, the api, and the package that writes a person's file) is drawn in the Decide section of a run's own page. Nothing of it is on the Factory, which is where a person watches. This is the first slice of the "needs you" surface of docs/pivot-2026-09-18.md (§6, P9).

Read CLAUDE.md (the apps/web lines — React Aria, `routeLink`, the e2e's rules, *A card on the Factory must fit its cell*, *Something beside the Factory's floor…*, and: a run's decision and an in-flight transcript are read on a run's DETAIL, never on the list the Factory polls), then apps/web/src/components/Gate.tsx and Decide.tsx, the Factory's components (the run's card, the step node, the step inspector and its tabs), apps/web/src/lib/factory.ts (`CELL_HEIGHT`, placement), apps/web/src/lib/api.ts (`decisionQuery`, `approveStep`, `askTry`, what the runs LIST carries against what a run's detail carries), apps/api/src/app.ts (the list the Factory polls, a run's detail, its decision), and apps/web/test/e2e/run.ts (the `decide` and `answer` pages, `expectClearOfEachOther`).

What to build:

1. WHAT IS ASKED, in a person's words, wherever a gate is shown. For a step at its gate, the inspector opens with it, above everything else: which step waits, what approving does ("approving runs ship: a trial merge of <branch> onto <base>, this workflow's check, and a commit — or an abort that says why"), and what sending back does and to which step (`sendBackTo`). Then the facts a person decides from, each from the record and none from anyone's account: the round the run is on and its cost, the last review's word (asks no further round, or what it left, as a count with a link to the findings), the check's verdict on the final try, what the run touched, and whether the result branch still merges onto the base — read on the DETAIL the inspector already fetches, never on the polled list. The run's card says the same in one or two lines that fit, not a sentence cut off mid-way; the full text lives in the inspector.

2. THE ANSWER, where the question is. Approve and Send back on the gate's node or its inspector, and a compact pair on the run's card. Reuse Gate.tsx's hooks and words; there is still one writer, the api through the package. Send back requires words; Approve takes optional ones. An approval at an ending merges onto the base branch, so one stray click must not do it: a second, deliberate press confirms it (a React Aria dialog or a two-step button — never `window.confirm`; CLAUDE.md on dialogs in the e2e). After an answer the card says what happened and what comes next ("approved — the next pass files ship"), and a refusal from the api is shown in the api's words.

3. THE INSPECTOR of a step that has not been filed says so once ("not filed yet: it waits at its gate") rather than printing a table of dashes. The spec stays below. Do not redesign the inspector beyond this; say in features.md what is still a mess in it and why you left it.

4. The other reasons a run waits on a person — a step signed off `blocked` with a `needs`, a finished run at its result — get the same first part only: what is asked, in words, on the card and in the inspector, with a link to where it is answered on the run's page. Inline actions are for gates in this change.

5. The e2e draws it, in both themes: a seeded run at an ending's gate, the card and the inspector read, an approval confirmed, a send-back with words, a refusal on a page of its own (the watch counts every 4xx), the store put back before each page ends, and every card still clear of its neighbours after a Reset layout (`expectClearOfEachOther`).

Not part of this change: a "needs you" page or queue of its own; notifications; agents, teams or dispatch; anything in packages/agent-engine. No change to packages/agent-workflows is expected; if the detail lacks a fact you need, add it to the api from what the package already returns, and say so.

You own apps/web and apps/api, with their tests and the e2e, and packages/agent-workflows/docs/features.md for the account of it. Done means `npx turbo run test --force`, `npm run typecheck`, `npm run build` and `npm run e2e` green from the root, and the PNGs read in both themes with the look skill — name in your closeout the first thing a person would notice on the Factory with a gate waiting. Commit as you go, plain messages, no attribution trailers. End your own servers and watchers before you sign off.
