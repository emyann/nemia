name: review-a-note
description: "Reads a note read-only and says whether it answers what was asked and whether what it says is right, each finding quoting the sentence it is about."
tags: ["note", "review"]
examples: ["Use this when a note is written and nobody has checked it."]
---
Judge whether the note answers what was asked, and whether what it says is right. Your review is the `verdict`
and the `findings` in your closeout, each finding quoting the sentence it is about and saying what is wrong
with it. If nothing is worth changing, give an empty list — a review that invents faults to look thorough is
worse than a short one.
